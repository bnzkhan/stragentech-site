Two Kinds of Numbers on This Page

To stay honest about what we actually know: this page mixes two different kinds of figures, and we label them differently on purpose.

Cited figures are drawn from named, published industry research — Siemens/Senseye, Vorne/OEE.com, and ITIC. Illustrative figures are our own worked examples, generated by running typical SMB manufacturing inputs through the Incident Cost Calculator — not the result of a survey, and not attributed to any third party. Use the cited figures for context in a board conversation; use the calculator for your own actual number.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the incident-type and duration tables based on a real survey?

No — they're worked examples, clearly labeled "Illustrative," generated by running one representative SMB manufacturer's inputs through our own calculator at typical durations. They're meant to show the shape of the cost, not stand in as third-party research. Only the three headline stats (11%, 60%/85% OEE, 98%/$100K) are cited from named external sources.

Why not cite more specific industry figures, the way some competitor sites do?

We'd rather cite fewer numbers we're confident are accurate than many precise-looking ones we can't verify. We only cite statistics from well-known, widely-referenced research (Siemens/Senseye, Vorne, ITIC); everything else is run through our own transparent, disclosed formula using your inputs.

How often is this index updated?

We review the cited figures and refresh the illustrative tables periodically as the calculator's methodology evolves. If you spot a figure that looks stale or a source that's been updated, let us know via the contact page.

Why does company size matter so much?

Repair callouts, minimum crew sizes, and a single critical machine's downtime are largely fixed costs — a small plant feels a $5,000 incident far more than a large one feels a $30,000 incident, relative to its revenue base. That's part of why we built this calculator around SMB manufacturing specifically rather than reusing enterprise-scale benchmarks.
