Questions

Before we talk

Straight answers to what most leaders ask on the first call.

What does a fractional CTO actually do?

The same job as a full-time CTO, sized to what you need. We own technology strategy, architecture, vendors, security and the AI roadmap, lead whoever builds for you, and report to you in plain English. You get senior judgment a few days a month instead of a full-time executive salary.

We're not a technology company. Is this for us?

Yes. Most of the value is in companies whose product isn't software: the work runs on people, spreadsheets and a handful of systems, and that is exactly where AI and better technology decisions pay back fastest.

Do you build, or only advise?

Both, in that order. We start with a roadmap so you know what is worth building. Then we lead the build, delivered by your team, by us, or with vetted build partners depending on scope. Either way you own the code, the data and the IP.

How is pricing structured?

By deliverable and outcome, not by the hour. The AI Roadmap is a fixed fee, fractional leadership is a monthly retainer, and builds are project-priced. You see the number before any work starts.

Which AI tools and platforms do you use?

We're vendor-neutral. We start from the workflow and choose what fits, which is often something you already pay for. Where it matters, we set things up so you own your agents and data rather than renting them.

How quickly will we see results?

The roadmap takes two to three weeks. The target for the first working asset in production is within 60 to 90 days of starting.

Our data and systems are a mess. Should we fix that first?

That is the first step of the work, not a reason to wait. We find the specific fixes AI depends on and do those, rather than launching a multi-year cleanup.

How do you handle security, compliance and risk?

Our background is in regulated, safety-critical systems. Every roadmap names the no-go zones first, keeps a person in the loop for consequential decisions, and leaves an audit trail.
